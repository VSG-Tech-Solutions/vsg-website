Does Core change anything in our ERP?

No. VSG Core integrates with your ERP without changing anything in it. Switch it off and your ERP is exactly as it was.

How current is the data?

Core refreshes automatically on a schedule agreed with your team, so every dashboard and report shows the latest figures from your ERP and existing systems.

Where does our data live?

On your own server, or with South African hosting. Your data stays in South Africa and is never pooled with anyone else’s.

Do custom reports come with Core?

Yes. We build the reports and dashboards your business needs on Core, in your format. A report delivered to your inbox every week with a written summary is a VSG Endorse automation.

Can we look up one customer and see everything?

Yes. Every customer, supplier and item has its own page: orders, balances and full history. Your team sees only what their role allows.

How does VSG Core fit into VSG ACE?

Core is the foundation of ACE and is included in every deployment. Core is the first layer to go live, so your reports and dashboards can start there, and every AI automation and application added to ACE works from the same numbers.

Does it work with our ERP?

Yes. VSG Core integrates with your ERP and existing systems, including your spreadsheets. We set up the connection for you, and nothing in your ERP changes.
