1What cookies are

A cookie is a small file that a website saves in your browser, for example to keep you signed in or to follow your visits. Browsers can also store information in other ways, such as local storage.

2What this site stores

Nothing. This site sets no cookies and uses no local storage, session storage or other browser storage.

It has no analytics, advertising or social media tags, and no embedded videos, maps or chat widgets.

3Services your browser contacts

To show our typefaces, your browser downloads them from two font services:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), run by Google LLC, for IBM Plex Mono.
  • Fontshare (api.fontshare.com and cdn.fontshare.com), run by the Indian Type Foundry, for Switzer.

Like any website your browser visits, they receive your IP address, your browser details and the address of our site. When we checked on 27 September 2026, neither set a cookie. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

When you send one of our forms, it goes to our own website and is emailed to us. The privacy policy explains what happens to it.

4Why there is no cookie banner

Under POPIA, electronic direct marketing needs your consent, and the Information Regulator’s guidance lists the use of cookies among the methods it covers. This site sets no cookies and runs no tracking, so there is nothing to ask your consent for.

If we ever add cookies or analytics that need your consent, we’ll ask before they run and update this policy first.

5The copy button

On the contact page, the Copy button puts our email address on your clipboard when you press it. It doesn’t read your clipboard or store anything.

6Controlling cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser’s settings. Doing so won’t affect this site, because it doesn’t use any.
