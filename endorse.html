Routine checks take up your team’s week. Costly problems still slip through.

  • A top-selling item runs out in 9 days.

    The next delivery is 21 days away. Without a reorder today, sales are lost.

  • Supplier invoices R 38 610 above the order.

    Five invoices priced above the order or billed for more than arrived. Without a check, they are paid in full.

  • A stock count R 48 730 short.

    The same items came up short three counts in a row, a pattern buried in the reports.

  • Three regular customers stopped ordering.

    Together they bought R 410 000 last quarter. The drop went unnoticed for six weeks.

Frequently asked questions.

Another question? Speak to our team.

Contact us
What if the job we want automated is not on this page?

We build it. The automations on this page are examples. Tell us about the task and we will help you decide what it takes to automate.

Do we have to start with all of them?

No. Start with the one that costs you the most and add more when you are ready.

Does the AI make decisions on its own?

No. AI finds the issue, works out its value and drafts the work. A person approves, rejects or edits every action, and their name goes in the decision log. Only alerts and reports for your own team are sent directly.

Does it change anything in our ERP?

No. VSG Endorse integrates with your ERP and existing systems without changing anything in your ERP. Endorse returns alerts, reports, draft emails and files, and your team decides what to do with each.

Do we need our own AI team?

No. We find the jobs, build each automation, connect it to your ERP and the other systems your team uses, and keep it running. We test it on your own real cases before it goes live.

Can it send us alerts and reports?

Yes. Alerts and scheduled reports are automations too, sent straight to the person responsible: stock about to run out, a customer over their credit limit, an order stuck for three days, a weekly management pack written by AI. Anything addressed to a customer or supplier waits for approval.

Does it email our customers?

Only with approval. Endorse drafts the email, and a person reads and sends each one.

How do we know it works?

Every decision is logged, every number traces to its source, and the monthly results report measures the real change in your ERP, automation by automation.

How is it priced?

Each automation has a fixed price, agreed before any work starts. We quote it after a VSG Bootcamp or a short call to scope the work.
