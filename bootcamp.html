Frequently asked questions.

Have another question? Speak to our team.

Contact us
Do you change anything in our ERP?

No. We integrate with your ERP and existing systems without changing anything in them.

Where does our data go?

On your own server or South African hosting. Your data stays in South Africa, handled in line with POPIA.

How much of our team's time does it take?

About two hours a day from your finance or operations lead, and a few hours each from two or three others across the week. The first morning and the final presentation matter most.

Where does AI come in?

Twice. On day three, AI analyses thousands of your transactions and surfaces every problem worth fixing, and our team costs each one. On day four, we build one AI automation that ranks the work by value and drafts the fix. Your team approves every decision.

Do we need a particular ERP?

No. We integrate with your ERP and existing systems. Tell us what you use and we will confirm how the week works for your setup.

What happens after the Bootcamp?

You receive a fixed quote for phase one. That might be an automation in VSG Endorse, VSG Procure or a custom build. We will help you decide, with no obligation.

Is it worth it if we do not go ahead?

Yes. The costed list of every issue, the map of your key processes and the fixed quote are yours to keep, to act on with us or without us.

What does it cost?

One fixed price for the week, agreed with you before anything is booked, and credited against phase one if you go ahead within 30 days.
