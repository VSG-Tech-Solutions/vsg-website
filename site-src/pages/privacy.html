1Who we are

VSG Tech Solutions (Pty) Ltd ("VSG", "we", "us", "our") is a private company registered in South Africa, registration number 2026/364466/07. We build custom software, AI systems, and products for the South African mid-market. Our registered office is in Cape Town.

We're the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) for the personal information we collect through this website, our forms and our emails with you. Our Information Officer is Stephan Esterhuizen, our CEO. For any privacy question, email stephan@vsgtech.co.za.

2What this policy covers

This policy covers personal information we collect through:

  • Our website (vsgtech.co.za), including any subdomains.
  • Our contact form, our Bootcamp booking form, and email correspondence.
  • Calls and meetings you book with us, online or in person.

It does not cover personal information we process for a customer inside that customer's own systems or its deployment of our products. There, the customer is the responsible party and we are its operator: we process that information only on the customer's instructions, as POPIA requires.

3What we collect

Information you give us. When you fill in a form, book a Bootcamp or email us, we collect your name, your work email and what you write in your message. You can also give us your company, job title and phone number; those fields are optional. The contact form records the topic you choose, and both forms record that you ticked the consent box, with the date and time.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the site, our hosting provider (Vercel) records your IP address, browser type, the page you came from and the pages you visit. This is standard server logging, used to keep the site reliable and secure. Your browser also downloads the site's fonts from two font services, Google Fonts and Fontshare, which gives them your IP address and browser details (see section 10).

What we don't collect. We don't use analytics or advertising trackers, social media pixels such as the Facebook Pixel or the LinkedIn Insight Tag, or anything similar, and we don't sell your data. If we ever add analytics, we'll update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

4Why we collect it

We only use your personal information for these purposes:

  • To reply to your enquiry or set up the Bootcamp, demo or call you asked for.
  • To send you the proposal, scope, or follow-up you've asked us to send.
  • To keep records of our engagements for accounting, tax, and legal reasons.
  • To keep the website running, debug issues, and prevent abuse.

We will not email you marketing material you didn't sign up for. If we ever start a newsletter, it will be explicit opt-in and you'll be able to unsubscribe with one click.

5Legal basis (POPIA)

Under POPIA, we process your personal information on the following grounds:

  • Consent: you tick the consent box when you send one of our forms, or you choose to email us. You can withdraw your consent at any time; that doesn't affect what we did before you withdrew it.
  • Performance of a contract: to deliver the work you've engaged us for.
  • Legitimate interests: to run our business, keep records, and improve our products.
  • Legal obligation: where we're required by South African law to retain certain records (for example, tax records under the Tax Administration Act).

6Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. Ever. We share it only with service providers that process it on our behalf, under their own data protection terms, and only as far as each one needs it:

  • Vercel Inc. (USA): website hosting, and the function that sends our forms.
  • Resend (USA): delivers form messages to our mailbox by email.
  • Microsoft 365: our work email and document storage.
  • Our accountants and auditors: only when legally required.

Two font services also receive your IP address and browser details when your browser downloads the site's fonts: Google Fonts (Google LLC, USA) and Fontshare (Indian Type Foundry, India). They are not our operators, and their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

7Where it goes (cross-border transfer)

Vercel, Resend, Microsoft, Google and Fontshare may store or process information outside South Africa, for example in the European Union, the United States or India. Section 72 of POPIA allows this when the recipient is bound, by law or by binding terms, to protect the information to a standard substantially similar to POPIA's, or when you agree to the transfer. We use services that commit to protecting personal information in their data protection terms, and our forms tell you about the transfer before you send anything.

8How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it:

Unsuccessful enquiries
12 months, then deleted.
Active customer records
For the life of the engagement plus 5 years.
Tax and accounting records
5 years from the end of the relevant tax year (as required by SARS).
Copies at our email service
Kept by Resend for a limited period under its own retention settings, then deleted.
Hosting logs
Kept by Vercel for a short period, then deleted automatically. If a form message can't be emailed to us, it is written to these logs and deleted with them, which is why the form asks you to email us as well.

9Your rights

POPIA gives you the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct anything that's wrong or outdated.
  • Ask us to delete your personal information (subject to our legal retention obligations).
  • Object to processing, or withdraw consent at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

To exercise any of these rights, email stephan@vsgtech.co.za. We'll respond within 30 days. There is no charge for reasonable requests.

You can reach the Information Regulator through its website, inforegulator.org.za, where complaints are lodged, by email at enquiries@inforegulator.org.za, or by phone on 010 023 5200.

10Cookies

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. When we checked on 27 September 2026, the two font services it uses, Google Fonts and Fontshare, set no cookies either. So there is nothing to accept and no cookie banner. Our cookie policy has the details.

11Security

We take reasonable, technically appropriate measures to protect your personal information. The site is served over HTTPS, our hosting provider runs in SOC 2-audited data centres, and access to our internal systems is restricted to people who need it and protected with strong authentication.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach that affects your personal information, we'll notify you and the Information Regulator as required by Section 22 of POPIA.

12Children

Our website and products are aimed at businesses, not children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

13Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of the page, and, where the change is significant, we'll email customers and prospects whose contact details we hold.
