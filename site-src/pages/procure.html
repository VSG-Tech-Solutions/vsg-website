Spreadsheet buying costs more than it shows. Stock ends up in the wrong places.

In too many businesses, buying still runs on spreadsheets and one buyer’s memory. That holds until you stock thousands of items. Then best sellers run dry while slow movers gather dust and swallow cash. The import that looked cheapest turns out dearest once duty and freight arrive. Containers sail with space you paid for and never used. What that can cost:

  • Best sellers out of stock for weeks.

    Three weeks without one top seller can cost R 330 000 in lost sales. Customers buy elsewhere.

  • R 1.4 million tied up in stock that will not sell this year.

    Bought on gut feel and paid for in cash.

  • Suppliers chosen on the quoted price.

    Once duty, clearing and freight are added, the cheapest quote is often not the cheapest supplier.

  • Containers shipped 14% empty.

    The full freight bill is paid on space that carried nothing.

How it works. From your sales history to purchase orders.

Your ERP already knows what sold, what is on the shelf and what is on order. VSG Procure turns that into a buying plan for every item, and the plan into purchase orders. Three steps.

  1. 1. Integrates with your ERP

  2. 2. Forecasts every item

  3. 3. Your buyers plan and order

    Buyers set every quantity, request quotes, compare suppliers at the landed price and generate purchase orders ready to import into your ERP.

VSG Procure at a glance. Ten features that protect your cash and keep your best sellers on the shelf.

Every number starts from your own sales and your own stock. Procure integrates with your ERP and existing systems and does the arithmetic. Your buyers make every call.

  • Forecast every item

    Forecasts demand for every item from the last twelve months of your own sales, with December and January weighted at half.

    Why it mattersFaster decisions, because every buying plan starts from what your customers actually bought.

  • Months of cover, five months ahead

    Projects stock and months of cover for every item five months forward, counting every order already committed or planned.

    Why it mattersFewer stock-outs, because your buyers see a shortage coming while there is still time to act.

  • Run-out and overstock lists

    Items about to run out in one list. Items holding more stock than target in another.

    Why it mattersSharper control, as your buyers spend their time on the items that put sales and cash at risk.

  • Cash you can free up

    Totals the value, at cost, of all stock held above your target.

    Why it mattersCash freed for the business, with surplus stock valued and in plain view before the next order goes out.

  • Heavy buying months, seen early

    Sets each month’s planned and committed buying against an average month of sales.

    Why it mattersFirm control of cash flow, with heavy buying months visible before you commit more cash.

  • The buying pipeline, one screen

    Shows the whole buying pipeline on a single screen, where your buyers set every quantity themselves.

    Why it mattersFaster planning with full accountability, because every quantity is a decision your buyer made and can explain.

  • Requests for quote from the plan

    Creates requests for quote straight from the plan and imports every supplier reply without retyping a single price.

    Why it mattersTime back on every buying round, and fewer mistakes from manual capture.

  • Landed price comparison

    Compares every supplier side by side at landed price, using the exchange rate, duty, clearing, freight and other costs your buyer sets per quote.

Free up the cash on your shelves. Plan every month before you buy.

Surplus stock is cash on a shelf. VSG Procure values every unit above your target at what you paid for it, so you see exactly how much cash is tied up, and in which items. Three numbers keep every month of buying honest.

Cash above target
The value of stock above your target, at cost. Stop reordering it or sell it down, and the cash comes back.
Buy-up cash factor
Each month’s planned and ordered buying against an average month’s sales, so a heavy month shows before the cash goes out.
Stock-out risk
Items about to run out come first, so cash goes where it earns.

Frequently asked questions.

Have another question? Talk to our team.

Does Procure change anything in my ERP?

No. VSG Procure never changes anything in your ERP. Procure generates purchase orders as a file, and your team checks and imports them.

Does Procure create purchase orders?

Yes. Your buyers generate purchase orders straight from the plan, or after comparing quotes. Procure creates them as a file ready to import into your ERP, with stock code, warehouse, quantity, price, supplier, buyer and your reference on every line. Your team imports them. Procure never writes into your ERP.

How does the forecast work?

Procure averages each item’s last twelve months of sales, with December and January counted at half for the holiday slowdown, and projects stock five months ahead, including orders already placed or planned. Your buyers set the target and every order quantity.

What kind of stock does it handle?

Any stocked item you buy locally or import. We confirm the fit on your own range during the demo.

Where does it run?

On your own server or with South African hosting.

What does it cost?

A fixed price, set by the size of your range and your setup, agreed with you before any work starts. Not sure where to start? A five-day VSG Bootcamp puts a cost on every stock and buying issue first.
