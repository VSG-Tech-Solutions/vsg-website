What every custom application comes with. Every build runs on ACE, with the full platform underneath.

Your build does not start from an empty page. From the first phase, your application runs on the same foundation as VSG Core and VSG Endorse: your data organised, a single approval inbox and a full decision log.

  • Your data, connected and organised

    VSG Core integrates with your ERP, spreadsheets and existing systems on a set schedule and organises the data around your suppliers, stock, orders, customers and invoices.

    Why it mattersConsistent figures across every team, because your application runs on the same organised data as your dashboards and reports.

  • A single approval inbox

    Every action your application prepares for a customer or a supplier, from a drafted reply to a problem to check, waits in one approval inbox until the right person signs it off.

    Why it mattersFull control over what leaves your business, while your team spends its time approving work instead of preparing it.

  • A view for every role

    Each person opens straight onto the screens and numbers their role needs.

    Why it mattersLess time lost between systems, because every person opens straight onto the work that matters to their role.

  • A complete decision log

    Every decision is recorded with who made it, when, what was decided and which documents it was based on, and the full log exports on demand.

    Why it mattersAudit-ready accountability, so questions from auditors, directors and customers are answered from the record instead of from memory.

  • Live dashboards and reports

    VSG builds live dashboards, reports in your own format and a dedicated page for every customer, supplier and item.

    Why it mattersFaster decisions, because your managers see the state of the business without waiting for a report to be compiled.

  • AI insights in plain language

    Your dashboards carry AI insights that explain in plain language what changed and what needs attention.

    Why it mattersEarlier action on the issues that cost your business money, without hours spent searching through reports.

  • Alerts and scheduled reports

    Alerts and scheduled reports, such as a weekly management pack with a summary of what changed, arrive in your team's inboxes automatically.

    Why it mattersFewer surprises, because the right people receive the problems and results that matter to them without having to ask.

  • Click through to the source

    Click any number on any screen to trace it back to the source record behind it.

    Why it mattersFewer mistakes and faster agreement, because every team can verify a figure before acting on it.

Frequently asked questions.

Which ERPs do you work with?

Whichever ERP you run. Everything we build integrates with your ERP and existing systems, and we set up the connection as part of the build.

Where does the AI come in?

Wherever reading and checking take up your team’s day: emails, documents, statements and thousands of transaction lines. AI checks and drafts, and a person approves before anything is sent.

How is it priced?

A fixed price per phase, agreed before work on that phase starts.

Who owns what you build?

Ownership and licensing are agreed in the quote, before any work starts.

Who maintains it afterwards?

Support after delivery, or a full handover to your team, is agreed in the quote before any work starts.

Will it write anything into our ERP?

No. Everything we build integrates with your ERP through VSG Core and leaves your ERP exactly as it is. The output is a screen, a file, a report or a draft that your team reviews and acts on.
