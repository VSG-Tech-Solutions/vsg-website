VSG is an AI company
that specializes in operations.

Walk into Monday knowing what to reorder, which supplier invoices do not match what arrived and which customers have stopped ordering. We build AI for finance, stock, suppliers, sales and production, and your team approves every decision. Everything we build integrates with your ERP and existing systems.

Book a Bootcamp Contact us

Headquartered in Cape Town. Serving manufacturers, distributors, wholesalers and retailers across South Africa.

Our mission. Help every business run faster, leaner and smarter with AI.

Your business already knows what to buy, where margin slips away and which customers need a call. The answers are buried in invoices, stock records and sales history, split between the ERP and a dozen spreadsheets. We bring them together, put AI to work on them and hand your buyer, credit controller and managers a clear next move every morning. Your team makes the call.

  1. VSG ACEThe AI platform for operations: data, AI automations and applications on one platform, with a view for every role.
  2. VSG BootcampA five-day, fixed-price assessment at your premises: every issue costed from your own data, and a fixed quote for phase one.
  3. VSG ProcureProcurement planning software for businesses that hold stock: forecasts every item from your own sales, shows the cash tied up in excess stock and compares suppliers on the full cost delivered to your door. Integrates with your ERP and existing systems.
  4. VSG EndorseEvery AI automation in your business, each built for you. AI drafts the work, and nothing leaves your business without your team’s approval.
  5. VSG CoreYour business data, integrated with your ERP and existing systems: live dashboards, custom reports and a personal view for each person on your team. Included in every ACE deployment.
  6. Custom applicationsCustom AI systems, workflow automation and software built for the problems that slow your business down, delivered in phases, each at a fixed price.
