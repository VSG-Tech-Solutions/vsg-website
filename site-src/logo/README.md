# VSG logo: the wordmark

Decided by Stephan on 2026-09-25: the logo is the wordmark "VSG." on its own, with a cobalt full stop. No symbol.
Installed files: `assets/site/logo/wordmark.svg` (obsidian), `wordmark-light.svg` (white), `mark.svg` and `mark-light.svg` (the app tile), `favicon.svg` and `/favicon.svg`.
The partial `site-src/partials/wordmark.html` draws the logo inline. `site-src/partials/mark.html` draws the small tile used next to text in product screens (a rounded square with the wordmark inside).

## The wordmark

"VSG" outlined from Switzer Semibold, tight viewBox 42.5 5.8 55.5 20.4. Outlines only; no font file ships.
Nav height 22px, footer 24px. Clear space: the height of the V on every side.

## The tile (favicon, app icon, small badges)

A 32 by 32 rounded square (radius 8), obsidian #141625, with the wordmark in white, 24 units wide, centred.
On dark grounds: white tile, obsidian letters.

## Colour

Obsidian #141625 on light, white on dark. One colour only; never violet, never a gradient, shadow or outline on the flat logo.
